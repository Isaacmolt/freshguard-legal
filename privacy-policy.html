隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 2 月

概述

鮮守衛（FreshGuard）尊重您的隱私。本 App 的設計原則是將您的資料保留在您的裝置上，我們不會收集、儲存或傳輸您的個人資料到任何伺服器。

資料收集

我們不收集任何個人識別資訊。您在 App 中輸入的所有品項名稱、到期日期等資料，僅儲存在您的裝置本機及您的 iCloud 帳號中（若您啟用了 iCloud 同步功能）。

iCloud 同步

若您啟用 iCloud 同步，您的資料將透過 Apple 的 CloudKit 服務在您的裝置之間同步。此資料由 Apple 根據其隱私權政策進行管理，我們無法存取這些資料。

推播通知

本 App 使用本地推播通知提醒您品項即將到期。這些通知完全在您的裝置上排程處理，不經過任何外部伺服器。

廣告

免費版可能顯示 Google AdMob 提供的廣告。AdMob 可能收集匿名的裝置資訊用於廣告投放。Pro 版本不包含任何廣告。您可以參考 Google 的隱私權政策了解更多：https://policies.google.com/privacy

訂閱與購買

所有付款均透過 Apple 的 App 內購買系統處理。我們不會收集或儲存任何付款資訊。

第三方服務

除了 Apple iCloud 及 Google AdMob（免費版），本 App 不使用任何其他第三方服務。

兒童隱私

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下的兒童，也不會故意收集兒童的個人資訊。

政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將在本頁面公布。

聯絡我們

如您對本隱私權政策有任何問題，請透過 App Store 頁面與我們聯繫。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

FreshGuard respects your privacy. This app is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. All item names, expiry dates, and other data you enter are stored only on your local device and in your iCloud account (if iCloud sync is enabled).

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data will be synced across your devices through Apple's CloudKit service. This data is managed by Apple under their privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Push Notifications

This app uses local push notifications to remind you of items nearing expiry. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not pass through any external server.

Advertising

The free version may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymous device information for ad targeting. The Pro version contains no ads. See Google's privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Subscriptions & Purchases

All payments are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

Other than Apple iCloud and Google AdMob (free version), this app does not use any other third-party services.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our App Store page.
