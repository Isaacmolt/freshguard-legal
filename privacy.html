隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 2 月 24 日

概述

FreshGuard（鮮守衛）是由 YUNG HUNG WANG（以下簡稱「我們」）開發的 iOS 應用程式。我們非常重視您的隱私，本隱私權政策說明我們如何收集、使用及保護您的資訊。

資料收集與使用

FreshGuard 的所有資料（包括您新增的品項名稱、到期日、空間設定等）均儲存於您的裝置本機，我們不會將您的個人資料傳輸至任何外部伺服器。

廣告

免費版本的 FreshGuard 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能會收集裝置識別碼和廣告相關資料，以提供個人化廣告體驗。您可以透過 iOS 設定中的「追蹤」選項管理廣告追蹤偏好。Pro 版本不包含任何廣告。

訂閱與購買

FreshGuard Pro 訂閱透過 Apple 的 App Store 內購機制處理。所有付款資訊由 Apple 直接管理，我們無法存取您的付款資訊。

第三方服務

本應用程式使用以下第三方服務：

- Google AdMob（廣告投放）
- Apple StoreKit（應用程式內購買）

這些服務有各自的隱私權政策，建議您參閱 Google 和 Apple 的相關政策。

兒童隱私

FreshGuard 不針對 13 歲以下的兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資訊。

政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將在此頁面公佈，建議您定期查閱。

聯絡我們

hd@lab-tw.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Overview

FreshGuard is an iOS application developed by YUNG HUNG WANG ("we", "our"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Data Collection and Use

All data in FreshGuard (including item names, expiration dates, and space settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit any personal data to external servers.

Advertising

The free version of FreshGuard uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and advertising-related data to provide personalized ad experiences. You can manage your ad tracking preferences in your iOS Settings under "Tracking". The Pro version does not include any advertisements.

Subscriptions and Purchases

FreshGuard Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system. All payment information is managed directly by Apple, and we do not have access to your payment details.

Third-Party Services

This application uses the following third-party services:

- Google AdMob (advertising)
- Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google's and Apple's respective policies.

Children's Privacy

FreshGuard is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
